Sightline lets you build and tune your multi-monitor setup **from inside the game**. Instead of editing `multimon_config.sii` in a text editor and restarting, you move sliders and drag your views on the screen, and every change shows up immediately.

## What you can do

- **Edit every view live**: position, size, heading, pitch, roll, field of view, camera offset, and whether the cab interior is drawn.
- **Drag views on the screen.** Each view is outlined over the game. Move it, resize it, and let its edges snap to your monitors, the other views and the HUD.
- **Use AMD Eyefinity or NVIDIA Surround.** Tell Sightline how many screens you have side by side and views snap to where they meet.
- **Build a panorama** of 2 to 4 views, up to a full 360° around the truck.
- **Place the HUD** anywhere, or across the whole screen with one click.
- **Save profiles** and switch between layouts in a second. Share them as plain `.sii` files.
- **Stay safe.** Your configuration file only changes when you press **Save**, every Save keeps a backup, and deleted profiles go to the Recycle Bin.

## Who it is for

Anyone using the game's custom multi-monitor mode, whether that is three screens around your seat, a single ultrawide, or one monitor where you want a better field of view or an extra view in a corner.
