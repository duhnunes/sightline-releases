Sightline lets you build and tune your multi-monitor setup **from inside the game**. Instead of editing `multimon_config.sii` in a text editor and restarting, you drag your views on the screen and move sliders, and every change shows up immediately.

## What you can do

- **Edit every view live**: position, size, direction, field of view, camera position, and whether the cab and the world are drawn. Values are shown in plain units: percent, degrees and meters.
- **Drag views on the screen.** Each view is outlined over the game. Move it, resize it, and let its edges snap to your monitors, the other views and the HUD.
- **Use AMD Eyefinity or NVIDIA Surround.** Tell Sightline how many screens you have side by side and views snap to where they meet.
- **Start from a template** or **build a panorama** of 2 to 4 views, up to a full 360° around the truck.
- **Place the HUD** across the whole window or on the center screen with one click.
- **Save profiles** and switch between layouts in a second. Share them as plain `.sii` files.
- **Undo anything** with :kbd[Ctrl + Z], and put any field back with its reset button.
- **Stay safe.** Your configuration file only changes when you press **Save**, every Save keeps a copy of the old file, and deleted files go to the Recycle Bin.

## Who it is for

Anyone using the game's custom multi-monitor mode, whether that is three screens around your seat, a single ultrawide, or one monitor where you want a better field of view or an extra view in a corner. Sightline turns that mode on for you.
