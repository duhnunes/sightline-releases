Both are on the **Start from...** page. They replace your current views, after a confirmation. **Undo** brings the old ones back, and nothing is written to your file until you press **Save**.

## Templates

Each template is a row with a drawing of its views. Click **Use** (or double-click the row) and confirm in the row:

| Template | Layout |
| :--- | :--- |
| **One view** | A single view across the whole game window. |
| **Three screens** | Left, centre and right views, from the game's own triple-screen settings. |
| **Three screens with dashboard** | The game's triple-screen layout with a dashboard view. |
| **Empty** | No views at all. Add your own with **+** on the **Layout** page. |

Sightline puts the HUD across the whole window after applying a template. See [HUD position](./03-hud.mdx) to change it.

## Panorama

A panorama is a row of views, each turned so that together they cover part of the horizon. The middle of the screen always looks straight ahead.

| Setting | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Views** | How many views, from 2 to 4 |
| **Total angle** | How much of the horizon they cover together, up to 360° |
| **Each view covers** | Shown for you: the total angle divided by the views |
| **Camera sideways**, **up / down**, **forward / back** | Moves every view's camera, in metres |
| **Hide the cabin** | Turns off the cab interior in every view |

**Preview** updates as you change the values: on the left, the views on your screens; on the right, the views seen from above, with the truck facing up and one wedge for each view. Click **Build panorama** to create it.

### Examples

:::tabs

### 360° street view

All the way around the truck, from above the cab:

- **Views**: 4
- **Total angle**: 360°
- **Camera up / down**: 3 m
- **Hide the cabin**: on

### Three screens

A starting point for three screens around your seat:

- **Views**: 3
- **Total angle**: 180°

Then fine-tune each view on the **Layout** page.

:::

> [!WARNING] Performance
> Every view is drawn separately, so 4 views cost roughly 4 times the GPU work of one.

> [!NOTE] Bent lines at the seams
> Straight lines, such as roads or power lines, bend where two views meet. That is expected: each view is a flat camera pointing in its own direction.

A single view cannot cover 179° or more, so for example 360° needs at least 3 views.
