Both tools are in **Add monitor / start from a template**. They replace your current layout, after a confirmation. As always, nothing is written to your file until you press **Save**.

## Templates

Pick one in **template** and click **Apply template**:

| Template | Layout |
| :--- | :--- |
| `empty` | No views. Add your own. |
| `one` | A single wide view. |
| `three` | Three views side by side, built from your `r_multimon_*` settings. |
| `three_with_dash` | Like `three`, plus a view for the dashboard. |

Sightline puts the HUD across the whole screen after applying a template. See [HUD position](./03-hud.mdx) to change it.

## Panorama

A panorama is a row of views, each turned so that together they cover an angle of the horizon. The middle of the screen always looks straight ahead.

| Setting | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **views** | How many views, from 2 to 4 |
| **total angle** | How much of the horizon they cover together, up to 360° |
| **camera offset** | The same `camera_space_offset` for every view (x right, y up, z back) |
| **hide the cabin** | Turns off the cab interior in every view |

Below the sliders, Sightline shows how many degrees each view covers. Click **Build panorama** to create it.

### Examples

:::tabs

### 360° street view

All the way around the truck, from above the cab:

- **views**: 4
- **total angle**: 360
- **camera offset**: `0 3 0` (3 up)
- **hide the cabin**: on

### Three screens

A starting point for three screens around your seat:

- **views**: 3
- **total angle**: 180

Then fine-tune each view in its tab.

:::

> [!WARNING] Performance
> Every view is drawn separately, so 4 views cost roughly 4 times the GPU work of one.

> [!NOTE] Bent lines at the seams
> Straight lines, such as roads or power lines, bend where two views meet. That is expected: each view is a flat camera pointing in its own direction.

A single view cannot cover 179° or more, so for example 360° needs at least 3 views.
