title: On-screen editor
description: Move and resize views by dragging them over the game, with snapping.
order: 1
---

With the window open, every view is outlined on the game screen. The selected view is highlighted in cyan with its name, position and size in percent; the others show a thin outline with their name. A yellow bar along the bottom edge shows where the HUD is.

> [!NOTE]
> The outlines are drawn only while the Sightline window is open. Turn them off with **Show the views** on the **Settings** page.

## Select a view

Click a view on the screen to select it. Its tab opens on the **Layout** page. A click without dragging only selects; it never changes anything.

## Move a view

Drag the **inside** of a view. It moves live, and the game follows smoothly.

## Resize a view

Drag an **edge** or a **corner**. While you drag, only the outline changes. The new size is applied when you **release** the mouse.

Hold :kbd[Ctrl] to resize from the center: the opposite edge moves the same amount the other way, so the view stays centered where it is.

> [!NOTE] Why only on release?
> The game rebuilds a view's image every time its size changes, which makes it stutter for a moment. Applying the size once, on release, avoids a stutter on every mouse movement.

## Remove a view

Press :kbd[Delete] to remove the selected view. There is no confirmation: **Undo** (:kbd[Ctrl + Z]) brings it back, and nothing is written to your file until you press **Save**.

## Snapping

Edges stick when they come close to:

- the edges and the middle of the game window,
- the edges of your physical monitors,
- the edges of the other views,
- the left and right edges of the HUD,
- where your screens meet, if you use [Eyefinity or Surround](./06-eyefinity-and-surround.mdx).

A pink line shows what the view snapped to. Hold :kbd[Alt] while dragging to place a view freely.

## Positions and sizes

Positions and sizes are percentages of the game window:

- **Left** `0 %` is the left edge of the window, `100 %` the right edge.
- **Top** `0 %` is the **top** edge, and larger values move the view down.

Views stay inside the window while you drag them. Overlapping views are allowed; the game draws both.
