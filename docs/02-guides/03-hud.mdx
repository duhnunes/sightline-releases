title: HUD position
description: Choose where the HUD and the game's menus are drawn.
order: 3
---

The HUD (speedometer, mirrors, navigation) and the game's menus are drawn in one horizontal band of the window. Its position is set in **Game UI position**:

| Setting | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| `normalized_ui_x` | Left edge of the HUD (0 = left of the window) |
| `normalized_ui_width` | Width of the HUD, as a fraction of the window |

The yellow bar at the bottom of the screen shows the HUD's current span while the window is open.

## Full screen

**Full screen** puts the HUD across the whole window (`x 0`, `width 1`). This is Sightline's default: the templates and the panorama use it too.

> [!TIP] Three screens around you?
> With three screens, the game puts the HUD on the centre third so the gauges and mirrors stay in front of you. To do the same, set `normalized_ui_x` to `0.3333` and `normalized_ui_width` to `0.3333`.

## Keep the HUD inside the window

The HUD and the views must stay inside `0` to `1`. Sightline keeps the sliders in that range.
