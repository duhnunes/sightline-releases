The HUD (speedometer, mirrors, navigation) and the game's menus are drawn in one horizontal band of the window. Set it on the **HUD** page.

The yellow bar at the bottom of the screen shows the HUD's current span while the window is open.

## Quick places

| Button | Where the HUD goes |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Whole window** | Across the whole game window. Sightline's default: the templates and the panorama use it too. |
| **Centre third** | The middle third. With three screens, the gauges and mirrors stay on the screen in front of you. |

## Position

| Setting | Unit | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Left** | % | Left edge of the HUD, from the left of the window |
| **Width** | % | Width of the HUD |

The HUD must stay inside the window: **Left** plus **Width** cannot go past `100 %`.
