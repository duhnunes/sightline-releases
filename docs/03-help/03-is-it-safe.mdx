Sightline is free and closed source: its code is not published. So here is exactly what it does on your computer.

## What it does

- **It runs only inside the game.** It is a plugin in the game's own `plugins` folder, loaded by the game when it starts and gone when the game closes. Nothing is installed in Windows.
- **It uses the game's own multi-monitor commands**, the same `multimon` commands you can type in the game's console.
- **It writes only its own files**, in `Documents\<game>\.sightline\`, plus `sightline.ini` next to the plugin. See [Where Sightline keeps its files](../01-getting-started/03-first-steps.mdx#where-sightline-keeps-its-files).
- **It changes your `multimon_config.sii` only when you press Save or restore a backup**, and keeps a copy of the old file every time.
- **Deleted profiles and copies go to the Recycle Bin**, so you can get them back.
- **To restart the game**, after you turn the multi-monitor mode on or off, it saves your progress and closes the game, and a small helper waits for the game to close, asks **Steam** to start it again and exits.

## What it does not do

- **No internet.** Sightline never connects to anything. The only link, to this guide on the **Settings** page, opens in your browser when you click it.
- **No telemetry, no accounts, no data collected.**
- **It never blocks your wheel, pedals or controllers.** While its window is open, it takes only the mouse and keyboard, and gives them back to the game when you close it.

## Get it from the official page

Download Sightline only from its official page, **docs.duhnunes.dev/sightline**. Copies uploaded elsewhere may have been changed.

To remove it, see [Uninstalling](../01-getting-started/02-installation.mdx#uninstalling).
